Current projects

Your Planning Voice
In use

A tool that helps community groups draft planning objections grounded in planning policy. An AI model drafts, and rule-based checks stop anything the evidence doesn’t support. It has been used on three live cases (Carrington Moss, Lapwing Green Spaces, Mowbray BESS) and is developed with the Community Planning Alliance.

Research on English planning

My doctoral work audits 652 contested planning sites, drawn from 852 community submissions and cross-referenced against 65 government datasets. The main findings are:

  • Protection is uneven. Ecological protection is more common in more affluent areas, while heritage protection is more common in less affluent ones.
  • Farmland. Speculative infrastructure proposals concentrate on high-grade agricultural land.
  • Data gaps. Ecological records are thinnest in the commuter-belt fringe, and several national datasets were unavailable or restricted when I queried them.
  • Limits. I found no detectable difference in planning outcomes, so the work concerns the consultation stage rather than final decisions.
