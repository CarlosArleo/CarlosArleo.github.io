Empirical Foundation

Audit of English Planning

A two-year computational-statutory audit of the English statutory planning system: a federated spatial pipeline cross-referencing contested planning sites against 65 live open-government spatial layers. The canonical cohort is a locked, de-duplicated N = 652 contested sites, drawn from a gross harvest of N = 852 grassroots campaigns through a seven-stage sample-flow attrition funnel.

01
The Defensibility Gap — two axes
Statutory protection does not scale uniformly with wealth. Ecological shields skew affluent: Ancient Woodland buffer proximity returns OR = 4.88, p = 8.4 × 10⁻⁶. Built-heritage protection runs the opposite way: Heritage at Risk skews deprived at OR = 7.54, p = 3.2 × 10⁻⁵. The composite with the flood term removed holds at p = 0.0017. What the gap measures is a disparity in affluence-linked, preventative shields specifically — not statutory protection as a single category.
02
Speculative capital concentrates on the best farmland
Grid-scale energy capital intersects provisional Best and Most Versatile agricultural land at 72.7%; strategic residential sprawl at 54.2%; logistics at 50.0%; urban infill at 46.6%. Pearson's χ² on the 4 × 2 contingency table: χ² = 12.72, df = 3, p = 0.0053.
03
Ecological data scarcity peaks in the middle
Data deserts are non-monotonic across deprivation. 34.9% of contested sites sit in an Ecological Data Desert (fewer than 5,000 historical records within 1 km), peaking at 47.4% in IoD2025 decile 6 — the commuter-belt fringe. No detectable class difference overall: p = 0.34.
04
The state's own data infrastructure fails
Three services, three distinct failure modes, one vantage point, one day. HM Land Registry INSPIRE: scripted requests refused at the Cloudflare edge (403); a manual browser capture returned Service WFS is disabled. HSE Major Accident Hazard Pipelines: no national service found by the pipeline. Coal / MRA: configured addresses invalid (HTTP 200 carrying an "Invalid URL" error); the layer is discoverable through the public catalogue and answers correctly.
05
Labour constituencies carry the compound vulnerability
Of the 47 canonical Triple-Jeopardy sites (IoD2025 ≤ 3 and IMED ≤ 3), 41 (87.2%) fall in Labour-held constituencies versus 41.8% of non-TJ sites — a disparity of +45.4 pp, with OR = 9.51 [3.98, 22.73], Fisher's exact p = 7.4 × 10⁻¹⁰. Robust under whole-thesis Bonferroni.
06
The outcome is a clean null
Of 606 decided sites with a resolved NBN count, 546 permitted and 60 refused — a 90.1% developer success rate. Data-desert sites show no detectable difference in permission (OR = 0.98 [0.56, 1.71], Fisher p = 1.00); unresolved corporate ownership OR = 0.75, p = 0.34. The consultation-stage asymmetry does not translate into a detectable difference in the binary permit/refuse outcome — bounding the thesis to the consultation side.
